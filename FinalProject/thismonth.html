September Special: New Orleans




Welcome back to the blog! This month, me and my parents went to New Orleans for my mom's birthday. We've heard that the food scene in NOLA was one of the best in the country, so we decided to explore it. We tried out a lot of different food spots, and I'm excited to share them with you.




Cafè Du Monde

📍Multiple Locations

We're starting off with the best thing we had! It was the beignets from Cafe Du Monde. We arrived as soon as they opened and the beignets were so fresh. The powdered sugar was just right and the beignets were not too sweet. We had to get another order after we the finished the first one. This is a historical place and it's a must try. Out of all the beignets we had, this was a 11/10.

  • Vibes: Old-school charm that is bustling with energy
  • What to get: Beignets!!
  • Price: $1-10

Parkway Bakery & Tavern

📍538 Hagan Ave, New Orleans, LA 70119

Po'Boy (a.k.a poor boys sandwhiches) are a staple in New Orleans. We went here after we landed in NOLA and we were not dissapointed. We tried two flavors: the Wild Caught Gulf Shrimp and the James Brown. The former is the original po'boy with fried shrimp and the latter is a twist on the original with slow cooked BBQ. YOU NEED TO GET THE JAMES BROWN. It was so good and the BBQ sauce was not overpowering. It is recommened that you get po'boys "dressed", which means you get it with lettuce, tomato, and mayonaise. The bread was nothing special, but these sandwhiches are a must try.

  • Vibes: Local insitional spot with a bustling atmosphere
  • What to get: James Brown Po'Boy
  • Price: $10-20

Blue Oak BBQ

📍900 N Carrollton Ave, New Orleans, LA 70119

This is the best BBQ that we ever had. The brisket was so tender and the sauce was perfectly sweet. The jerked chicken wings were also a hit. It is a little far from the main tourist areas, but it is worth the trip. I would say that the serving size is a little small, but it is definitelty worth it.

  • Vibes: Indoor and outdoor seating with evening energy
  • What to get: Brisket and Jerked Chicken Wings
  • Price: $20-30

World Famous N'awlins Cafe & Spice Emporium

📍French Market

This was the highlight of the trip for my dad. We went here for the gator sausage (gator on a stick) our first day, but they were out of it. We went back the next day and they had it. It tasted better than chicken and it was a little spicy. Knowing that is a gator made it a little werid, but my dad loved it! He had a second one.

  • Vibes: Casual, no-frills atmosphere
  • What to get: Gator on a Stick
  • Price: $10-20

Loretta's Authentic Pralines

📍French Market

Like the gator sausage, we had to go back to get the pralines. These were the second beignets we had, and they were amazing. The pralines were a little sweet, but it is what made the beignets special. I would say that Cafe De Monde's beignets were better because of their dough, but these are a good 8.5/10!

  • Vibes: Sweet, nostalgic, and market-butsle atmosphere
  • What to get: Beignet filled Pralines
  • Price: $10-20

Acme Oyster House

📍Multiple Locations (Food Trucks)

More photos of my dad! He loved the chargrilled oysters. They were fresh and tasted unique. It was a little salty however. The fried seafood platter containg fried shrimp and fish were also good. The place was expensive for the small portions. It was a good experience though.

  • Vibes: Long lines with classic New Orleans Energy
  • What to get:Chargrilled Oysters
  • Price: $20-30

Willie Mae's NOLA

📍898 Baronne St, New Orleans, LA 70113

We went through a long journey to get here, but these are alledgely the best fried chicken in NOLA. The fried chicken was crispy! It was very busy and the chicken itself was oily. But the real star was the red beans and rice. It was recommened to us by the server and we were not disspaointed. The cornbread was also good.

  • Vibes: Modern comforts with soulful energy
  • What to get: Fried Chicken and Red Beans and Rice
  • Price: $20-30

Ayu Bakehouse

📍801 Frenchmen St, New Orleans, LA 70117

It was also a journey to get here, but I loved the atmosphere. We tried the chocolate babka, which is probably the best babka I've ever had. The chocolate was rich and the dough was soft. My dad also really liked their Americano. I wish I could of tried more of their pastries because it smelled so good.

  • Vibes: Sunday mornings with a cozy atmosphere
  • What to get: Chocolate Babka and Americano
  • Price: $10-20

Cafe Beignet

📍Multiple Locations

This was our first beignets and so we thought they were pretty good. They are, but compared to Cafe Du Monde and Loretta's, these were not as good. The beignets were a little dry and heavy to eat. They have a lot of locations, which made them accessible.

  • Vibes: French-cafe charm with jazz in the background
  • What to get: Beignets
  • Price: $10-20

Turkey and the Wolf

📍739 Jackson Ave, New Orleans, LA 70130

We saw this on a TV show and it is a very famous spot so we had to try it. It was a journey to get here, and it turned out to be average. We got the fried bologna sandwhich and it was okay. The bread was amazing, it was a brioche bread. Other than that, the mustard was too strong and the bologna was nothing special. Our expectations were high and were not met.

  • Vibes: Nostalgic with gamer vibes
  • What to get: Fried Bologna Sandwhich
  • Price: $10-20

French Truck Coffee

📍Multiple Locations (Food Trucks)

We tried the chicory coffee and the oji drip coffee. The chicory coffee is New Orleans' specialty coffee. It was just okay. The oji drip coffee was also just okay. The spot is nothing special.

  • Vibes: Friendly staff and modern vibes
  • What to get: Oji Drip Coffee
  • Price: $10-20
