My PCOS Journey


A few months ago, I was diagnosed with the hormonal disorder polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS). My adventures of trying out all the food spots came to a halt. I ended up stopping going to bakeries, as I was afraid for my body. Having PCOS means that I have high insulin resistance and my body can’t break down sugar and carbohydrates properly.

I was lost. I never knew about PCOS, as it’s never taught in school. However, I was inspired to research about it and ways to manage my symptoms. I found out that I didn’t have to cut the carbs and the food I love; it’s all about the balance. I took better care of myself and incorporated nutrient-dense foods. Slowly, my symptoms started to alleviate, and I felt better each day.

Yet, PCOS is a condition that is often stigmatized and poorly understood. I hope to raise awareness and inform more people about it, because it’s an issue that affects numerous women. I write this food blog with my passion and commitment to make a change.
